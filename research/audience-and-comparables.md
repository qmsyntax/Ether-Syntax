## 2. Audience

### This series (observed 2026-09-18) — HIGH
- **Royal Road**: 12,014 followers · 4,377 favourites · 2,661 ratings · score 4.57 · 998,428 views
  (understates lifetime readership — the fiction is stubbed to 36 chapters). Book 5 serialising.
- **Goodreads**: bk 1 5,948 ratings / 354 reviews at 4.19 · vol 2 4,174 · vol 4 2,422 →
  roughly 40% retention vol 1 → vol 4 by rating count.
- **Audible**: 3,756 / 2,554 / 3,080 / 2,478 ratings for bks 1–4; 16.5–23 h each.
- Audible counts ≈ 63% of Goodreads counts for bk 1 → audience looks audio-heavy. *Our inference, LOW–MED.*
- Kindle Unlimited; German edition exists.

### Genre demographics
- Similarweb model for royalroad.com, Aug 2026: ~68% male; largest band 18–24; US 38% then UK,
  Germany, France, Canada; video games a top audience interest. **MED** — modelled, not surveyed.
  https://www.similarweb.com/website/royalroad.com/
- NINC genre spotlight (working author's commentary): historically male, millennial/Gen X,
  gaming backgrounds; growing share of women; pipeline Royal Road → Patreon → Kindle Unlimited
  → Audible. **LOW–MED.** https://ninc.com/litrpg-a-genre-spotlight/
- Publishers Weekly, Oct 2024: no demographics; notes "superfans" buying multiple formats,
  audio as key format, Dungeon Crawler Carl at 800k copies by Apr 2024. **MED.**
  https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/new-titles/adult-announcements/article/96198-litrpg-goes-mainstream.html
- No subreddit census found. Forum age threads are anecdote (late 20s–30s).

## 4. LitRPG / web-serial VIDEO games (Sept 2026)

- **No licensed Western LitRPG or progression-fantasy PC/console game found shipped or announced.**
  MED — absence of evidence across several searches.
- Dungeon Crawler Carl: tabletop, a licensed pinball machine, and a 30-second promotional
  browser game (Jul 2026). The author has said he would "love" an MMORPG — aspiration, not a deal.
- Asian web-novel IP does get games, all big-studio and tied to anime/webtoon: Solo Leveling:
  Arise >$100M in three months on mobile (Sensor Tower estimate); its PC version 9.2k Steam
  reviews, Mostly Positive.
- Fan-made: a free Wandering Inn fan game has sat on itch.io for years, apparently tolerated. LOW–MED.
- **Blaise / Aethon stance on fan works: no public policy found.** MED. One data point — a long
  parody fan fiction is hosted openly on Royal Road, dedicated to the author. Non-commercial
  prose on the same platform is **not** a precedent for a game.

## 5. Session length and scope norms

- Runs: Halls of Torment markets 30 min, players report ~45 and ask for 15–20 (MED); Death Must
  Die 4–20 min (MED); Hades ~20–50 min from player reports (LOW–MED).
- Timelines: median ~2 years between releases across 2,966 multi-game Steam developers
  (survivorship-biased; the author recommends 6–12 months for new developers) — MED,
  https://gamemarketing.substack.com/p/how-long-is-too-long-to-spend-on . Solo/duo ARPGs took 4–8+ years.
