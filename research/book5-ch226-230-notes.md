## 1. Runes & abilities

Very thin in these four chapters; the rune system proper only arrives in ch230.
- [S] University class titled **"Advanced Blood Runeology"** exists and can be self-enrolled from the student tablet (ch228). [I] Runeology is taught **per affinity** and in **levels** (at least basic -> advanced), so the rune alphabet/grammar of ch230 has affinity-specific dialects or sub-catalogues.
- [S] "Forged Valor" is NOT an item or ability - it is the chapter title, a pun on badge forgery (ch228). "Overpearl" is the name of an ascension elixir, not a rune term (ch226, see s.3).
- [S] The mandatory catch-up list the empress imposes is 17 basic/refresher courses, including a one-student class covering primary- and middle-school content, created that same day by the head of the Curriculum Committee (ch228). [I] The empress's stated fear is that an uneducated fighter "builds wrong" and makes an irreversible mistake in his development (ch227) - consistent with ch230's idea that abilities are constructed and can be constructed badly.
- [S] Freddy ends up with 27 classes and ~7 free hours a day (ch228).
- [S] Ability-free attacks still count: a plain kick, powered only by the awakened soul construct's weapon-modding (no ability, no physique talent, ordinary shoes), one-shot a high-level three-star training doll (ch226). [I] Soul-construct empowerment applies to body parts as "wielded weapons", matching the ch203 wield-anything rule.
- [S] The construct-wrapped sword makes a screeching sound when it hits; the swing's force is something a four-star would have to respect (ch226).

## 2. Items & inventory

### Storage rings
- [S] Owner retrieves items by sinking attention into the ring; things can be stowed "deep in a corner" so they are unlikely to be pulled out by accident (ch226). [I] The ring has an internal layout / mental slots, and retrieval is intention-based rather than list-based - a mis-pull is a real risk with dangerous contents.
- [S] Things seen stored: cigarettes, badges, a sealed box, ID card, tablet/phone (ch226, ch229). [I] Everyday pocket items live in the ring; there is no "equipped vs bagged" distinction shown.
- [S] A powerful item inside a ring is undetectable from outside; the same item outside would broadcast to the whole neighbourhood (ch226). [I] Storage rings fully shield aura - relevant to theft, smuggling and detection mechanics.

### Ascension elixirs (the "Overpearl")
- [S] Overpearl = a knuckle-sized shimmering white orb kept in a small sealed black box; it radiates so much power that unboxing it in a residential area would alert everyone nearby (ch226).
- [S] It is a **true ascension elixir**, of unnamed and secret origin. Ordinary top-tier ascension aids (example: the **starfire mirror rose**, itself priceless) only inject **blank latent aura** - raw growth energy that inflates the soul; results are usually underwhelming and heavy elixir use has, in extreme cases, **downgraded people's talents** (ch226).
- [S] The Overpearl instead both pushes ascension AND amplifies the *quality* of the resulting evolution (ch226).
- [S] Handling rule given to him: never remove it from the storage ring except at the moment of eating it, then ascend immediately; do it inside an instance dungeon where nobody else can be present; it is worth killing for (ch226).
- [S] It was granted by the empire specifically to give him the best possible shot at breaking the Triple Block (ch226). He does not strictly need it - his soul healing is the only thing gating his ascension (ch226).
- [I] Two-tier elixir model for a game: common "blank aura" boosters (progress only, talent-degradation risk on overuse) vs rare "true" elixirs (progress + ascension quality bonus).

### Other items / tech
- [S] Recording crystals are slotted into a stand that transmits them - a one-way, high-privilege channel to the empress that only senior imperial officials receive and should use once, for something worth her time; success brings a big reward (ch226).
- [S] Phones are used to call school security / watchdogs (ch228). Videos of public incidents circulate within hours; Madame reviews the university confrontation on a screen the same day (ch229).
- [S] University tablet: schedule, enrolment, class catalogue with descriptions and host; attendance is anonymised on the public site (ch228).
- [S] Alchemists routinely use anti-aging tonics (a 60-year-old two-star looks like a student) and are the source of cutting-edge steroids (ch229).
- [S] A professor's focus tea triples study progress; ~40 minutes and several attempts to brew a cup (ch228).
- [S] Floating estates above Starhold start at ~$50B for the smallest (ch226) - refines the "~$40B liquid out of reach" line in ch219.

## 3. Essence, satellites, ascension

- [S] No new "satellite" information in ch226-229.
- [S] Aura pressure ladder confirmed: a trace of the empress's aura scrambles a two-star's thoughts far worse than Overlord Umbra's full presence did (ch227). Yet when she chose to, she gave off nothing at all - Freddy sensed no power through the wall (ch226). [I] Aura is fully suppressible at five-star.
- [S] **Insanity sense** (Freddy's danger radar) gave nothing on the empress because she did not regard her own break-in as dangerous or abnormal (ch226). [I] The sense reads the *approacher's own perception* of transgression/threat, not their power level - a stealth loophole for anyone who sincerely believes they are doing nothing wrong.
- [S] Five-stars have "impeccable" memory; two-stars have merely improved memory (ch227, ch229).
- [S] **Body durability scales with rank, and the nervous system scales fastest** - far outpacing other tissues. Chin strikes create brain torque that can KO or kill, but a high-level three-star's brain is expected to shrug that off (ch226). [I] Head/brain hits stay the kill condition; the "brain HP" multiplier grows faster than body HP per star.
- [S] Time-dilated dungeons make **subjective age** diverge from birth age: an information talent reads Freddy as "over 125" while his legitimate ID shows 25 (ch229). Tower of York has heavy dilation; the empire is presumed to hold instance dungeons with extreme dilation (ch229). [I] "Age" is a lived-time counter, and there is no penalty to the body for it if you have healing.
- [S] A youthful look is hard to maintain for long at two-star (ch229). [I] Aging slows but continues at two-star; only special talents/tonics stop it.
- [S] **Severing and re-ascending** is common enough that "severed powerhouse" is a recognised social type; a four- or five-star who severed back to the start to retry ascension is a normal explanation for an over-decorated two-star (ch226, ch229). Etiquette: severed veterans do not flaunt their medals (ch229).
- [S] Prime remnants come in grades; the empress has collected "countless" of them without ever getting a lucky result, whereas Freddy's construct came from a **unique** prime remnant he did not know was unique (ch227). [I] Remnant grade determines what the awakened construct becomes; unique = top grade; the outcome is partly luck.
- [S] The empress can touch a materialised soul construct and visibly considered ripping it out (ch227); whether that is possible is left open. The construct answers her aloud despite Freddy's plea for silence (ch227) - it does not obey the owner.
- [S] **Fate/wish mechanic, as the empress frames it**: the world responds to intensity of desire by generating opportunities (ch227). [I] Same engine as the "birth a fate" line in ch207/213.
- [S] Deadline: ascend to third star before **29 April 2225**, because "days of peace" end then; the empress wants a report on whether breaking the Triple Block helps (ch227). Today in-story is ~2220, so roughly five years.

## 4. Combat texture

- [S] What decided the evaluation fight (Lissia's review, ch226): he ate a crossbow bolt to the chest and lost an eye without flinching, deflected a bolt on timing alone, then killed with a construct-empowered kick to the chin. Ferocity, willingness to take any trade, and formless skill honed by real risk - not raw power. She calls him a machine for killing.
- [S] A four-star Lord-tier fighter puts her chance of losing to him at under 1% and says a two-star normally has no chance at all; the exceptions involve an ambush plus expensive spirit abilities (ch226).
- [S] Beating a monster above your own rank is a coin-flip at best; 100+ such kills is considered superhuman (ch228).
- [S] Real (non-arena) duels between students are apparently something you can propose in public; Freddy proposed the arena "so you don't die for good", the other side dared him to fight outside it, then backed off (ch229). [I] Lethal duels are legal/tolerated; the arena is the polite option.
- [S] Nervous-system durability means knockout mechanics scale with rank (ch226).

## 5. Society & institutions

### Badges - new tracks and rungs
- [S] **Guardian** medal: for fighting back against superior forces; his is special-rank, awarded for the defence of Repentawa overall, not just the Nahar kill (ch226).
- [S] **Entrepreneur** badge: issued via the bank; special-rank for earning enormously as an independent two-star; he did not know he had it because he never visited the bank (ch226).
- [S] **Adventurer** badge: counts *unique* dungeons completed; 150+ = fourth rank (ch226). Distinct from Dungeoneer (which counts clears).
- [S] **Slayer**: 100+ kills of above-rank monsters = fifth rank (ch228). With ch210's 720+ = special, that gives two rungs of the ladder.
- [S] **Martyr**: normally posthumous (you must die for the empire). Freddy wears a special-rank one (ch228). [I] His "spark of undeath" state was officially logged as a death.
- [S] Badges can go missing in bureaucracy and be recovered in an audit (ch226).
- [S] Badges are read as a coherent CV: fifth-rank Trooper (1M+ kills) beside first-rank Expert (<10 battles) reads as a forgery to any onlooker (ch228). The known loophole: using a talent or spirit ability to empower someone *from outside the battlefield* earns kill credit without participation credit (ch229). [I] The true cause is Bloodshed's kills being credited to the summoner (ch210) plus multi-day engagements counting as few "battles".
- [S] **Badge forgery is a capital crime**; people still try (ch228). Watchdogs can be pre-briefed so a real holder is not arrested (ch229).
- [S] The empress can order a person to wear every badge at all times; hiding them on her land is "cowardice" (ch227) - a reversal of Madame's keep-a-low-profile policy (ch223).
- [S] Too many special badges on a two-star draws instant challenges and accusations; most Lords have fewer special badges than Freddy (ch228).

### Empire, empress, Starhold
- [S] Empress **Kaiya**: over two metres, platinum suit, black cape with imperial insignia, peaked cap; can suppress her aura to nothing; breaks doors rather than knocking (ch226-227).
- [S] Reward package for his contribution: 560 elite project managers, 10,000+ workers (all at least two-star), and 70 watchdogs sent to the Northern Belt (ch226).
- [S] **Watchdogs** = the three-star counterpart of four-star adjudicators; they work in trios and arrive under a dense aura (ch226, ch228).
- [S] **Four-stars are not permitted to reside in the Northern Belt** (subordinate territory) (ch226). The Belt is meant to become a barrier against the **Frozen Wastes** (ch226).
- [S] Starhold sits under a **dome** with false sunlight; floating properties are stacking up beneath the dome and may one day block the light (ch226). The city is attacked practically daily; a big attack can gash the dome and level blocks; the empress personally handles the largest threats, and trouble comes when two arrive at once (ch226).
- [S] Countless four-stars live in Starhold but its safety rests on a handful of five-stars; the **Starhold Expansion Project** (new settlements) is failing because a settlement without a five-star cannot survive that scale of threat; the alternatives (mass fighters, traps, expensive weapons) are costly and logistically hard (ch226).
- [S] Senior imperial officials get a one-shot channel to the empress (ch226). She personally visits an "Overlord candidate" flagged this way.
- [S] **Sponsored ward of the empire**: full university tuition plus a large class-credit allowance; duties in power, culture and education; the empire's protection means nobody above his rank will dare touch him (ch227). She also discloses his identity to a Lord he wronged, deliberately, to see how he copes (ch227).
- [S] "Nova York" incident: Freddy, under the identity **Silver Heart**, killed children of Lords; the empress knows and does not care to punish it (ch227).
- [S] Most archhumans lose their drive and risk tolerance once they have comfort, some luxury and respect (ch226 - Freddy's reflection).
- [S] Universal surveillance culture: incidents in public halls are on video within hours (ch229).

### University of Starhold
- [S] Housed in a huge reinforced metal block near the city centre; the public entrance is a separate small building ~4 blocks away with registration booths, then conveyor-belt gates that carry students for minutes into the main building; the entrance hall has a 20 m ceiling and staff on flying platforms (ch227-228).
- [S] Registration: scan the imperial ID, receive a university ID card and tablet (ch227).
- [S] Open to anyone who can pay, no age rules (academies do have age rules); it is normal for accomplished people with poor schooling to attend (ch228).
- [S] **Free-market classes**: professors design and run whatever classes they like and earn mainly from class fees, so they chase high-attendance subjects; classes can be created same-day; a **Curriculum Committee** (head: Dr Jared Nocke) sets the craft of class design (ch228).
- [S] Costs seen: a scholarship student still pays ~$10M/year plus **class credits** on top, which she has to work to afford (ch228). Class credits are the per-course currency (ch227-228).
- [S] Study never ends: even professors keep taking classes as discoveries land; an alchemist trainee expects ~10 years to reach an entry-level company post (ch228).
- [S] Alchemy department uses a past-states vision talent to check ingredients (fruit age, bruising, impurities) at thousands of dollars per look; ingredients are worth fortunes; cheap, quick, non-destructive verification is otherwise rare (ch228).
- [S] Talent taxonomy used by alchemists: "true healing" vs "flawless injury reversal" (Georgie League named as a flawless-reversal case); supreme-quality healing implies no scars (ch229).
- [S] Anya's talent also works on people and documents (verified that an ID went through the legitimate issuing process); she hides that it works on people (ch228-229).

## 7. Contradictions / corrections to the ch203-225 notes

- **University pricing**: ch219 recorded "~$1B/year university access"; ch228 shows a scholarship student paying ~$10M/year plus credits. [I] Either the $1B figure was a premium/full-access tier or the scholarship covers ~99%. Treat university cost as tiered, not a single number.
- **Slayer ladder**: ch210 gave 720+ = special; ch228 adds 100+ = fifth. Not a contradiction, a missing rung.
- **Badge policy**: ch223's advice to hide badges is now overridden by an imperial order to wear all of them (ch227).
- **Insanity sense** (listed undefined in ch203-225): now has one observed rule - it fails against someone who does not think they are doing anything wrong (ch226).
- **Evaluation judge**: the "judge" of ch224-225 is Lissia, a four-star high adjudicator; the dummies were tuned to an average high-level three-star body (ch226).
- **Cycle Scythe origin**: now stated to come from a *unique* prime remnant (ch227), extending the ch224 note that awakening needs a prime remnant.
- **Martyr medal**: ch210 said "usually posthumous"; ch228 confirms Freddy holds a special-rank one, so a survivable qualifying "death" exists.

## 10. Design hooks (my suggestions, not canon)

- Storage as a mental space with "corners": deliberate deep-stowing costs retrieval time but prevents mis-pulls; dangerous items broadcast aura the instant they leave storage (detection/stealth tension).
- Two elixir tiers: blank-aura boosters (fast progress, talent-downgrade risk on overuse) vs rare true elixirs (progress + evolution quality).
- Badge CV coherence: incoherent badge sets trigger NPC suspicion/challenges; forgery is a capital-crime consequence; being ordered to display everything is a debuff to stealth and a magnet for duels.
- Danger sense that ignores "innocent" intent - lets unaware or self-righteous enemies bypass warnings.
- Subjective-age counter from time-dilated dungeons as a visible stat that NPC talents can read.
- Class credits as a spendable currency on a free-market course list; professors as vendors with their own catalogues.
- Head-hit lethality scaling: nervous-system HP grows faster than body HP per rank, so chin/skull hits stop being one-shot as targets rank up.
