**Subject:** A fan-built 1% Lifesteal game prototype — would you take a look?

Hi Robert,

My name is Brent [surname]. [One line about yourself: who you are, and how you came to the series, e.g. listening to the
audiobooks.] I've been a big fan of 1% Lifesteal, and over the last month I've been building a small game prototype set
in its world. I wanted to show it to you before it goes any further.

It's called *Passage* for now. It's a real-time isometric action RPG (think Diablo or Baldur's Gate 3 from above) in which
you go into passages, fight through the realms beyond, and come home to sell what you carried out. A few of the things
that make it feel like your world rather than a generic one:

- **Stars and the ethercosm.** You start with a baby star and grow it. Abilities sit in your ethercosm, where you bind
  vestiges to advance them, and a bind can fail.
- **The Netherecho.** You can step out of your body to gather wisps and fight constructs, while your body sits
  defenceless behind you.
- **Essence and reflux.** Every ability costs essence and leaves reflux behind. Run hot and everything costs more.
- **Talents are many.** 1% Lifesteal is one prime talent among about forty. Every player gets something different, and
  none of your characters appear in the game; everyone in it is original.
- **Death has weight.** You wake naked in town, and your body and everything on it lie where you fell until you go back
  for them.

The screenshots attached are from the current build: combat, a fire-affinity character, the ethercosm, the Netherecho,
the inventory and character creation. Some art is still placeholder.

To be clear about where it stands: this is a private prototype. It hasn't been released, shared or shown to anyone
else, no text from the books is in it, and I have no licence, so I'm writing to you first. If you'd be open to it, I'd
love to show you a build or talk about whether there's a version of this you'd want to see made. If game rights sit with
Aethon or someone else, I'd be grateful to know who to speak to. And if it's not something you want, I'll respect that
and set it aside.

Thanks for the books, and for reading this far.

Best,
Brent [surname]
