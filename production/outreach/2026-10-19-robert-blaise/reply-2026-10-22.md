## What it means for the project (our reading, not legal advice)

- **Fine by him:** a game *inspired by* the 1% Lifesteal universe that uses how the world works and the power system.
- **Not fine:** adapting the story or the characters. This was already a project rule.
- **Not a licence and not an adaptation.** Any official adaptation "begins on their side". This stays Brent's own
  creative project, and it shouldn't be presented as an official 1% Lifesteal game.
- **He'd like to see it.** That's an open door to share progress with him.
